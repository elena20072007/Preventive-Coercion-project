# Testing the DAPO: Hidden Risks of Preventive Coercion

This repository contains the article by **Elena Vassilieva** examining preventive coercion in British law, the Domestic Abuse Act 2021, and its implications for justice and human rights.

## Article
The full text is available on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/17393367).

## Project Description
This research investigates the emergence of preventive coercion as a unique legal phenomenon in British law. It analyses the institutional logic, legislative development, and societal implications, highlighting hidden risks to due process, fundamental rights, and the justice system. The study draws on empirical observations and a quasi-experimental engagement with the legal system.
